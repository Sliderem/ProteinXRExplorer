## Derived 3D model

`web/public/proteins/P01012/model.glb` was generated by `python/build_glb.py`: C-alpha atom coordinates are joined by a Catmull-Rom spline and swept into a tube of 1.1 angstrom radius (1 angstrom = 0.01 scene units), coloured with the standard AlphaFold pLDDT colours. No coordinates are altered other than centring. It is a visual backbone trace, not a cartoon. If you replace it with a GLB from Blender/Mol*, record its tool, version and settings here.

## What pLDDT means

pLDDT (0-100) is AlphaFold's per-residue confidence in the predicted position. It is not stability, not heat resistance, and not a measured property. The app never shows temperature-dependent structure changes.
